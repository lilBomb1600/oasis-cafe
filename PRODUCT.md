# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (TypeScript, App Router, src dir) + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + lucide-react. This is Arman Digital's established stack, reused unchanged across all 8 prior demo sites in this portfolio (Chaiwala Shop, The Dive, Little Red Bistro, Pittsburg Tires And Wheels, The Luxe Lounge, TRL BOYZ, Iron Therapy Gym, Feel Goods Barbershop) — not re-litigated per project.

## Users

Local Lafayette, CA residents and workers looking for a casual halal-friendly breakfast/lunch spot and specialty coffee drinks — checking the menu, hours, and location before a visit, or looking up whether the cafe is open/halal before walking or driving over. Secondary: this is also a portfolio/demo piece for Arman Digital, shown to the real Oasis Cafe owner as a pitch.

## Product Purpose

A real marketing/informational website for Oasis Cafe, an existing halal cafe in Lafayette, CA that currently has no website (menu and hours only exist as in-store photos and a Yelp listing). Success = the cafe has a real, live site that accurately represents its menu, hours, halal status, and location, usable both to serve real customers and as a pitch asset to close the business as a paying Arman Digital client.

## Positioning

A halal cafe with a distinctive Egyptian/desert-oasis theme running through its actual specialty drink menu (Sahara Sunset, Nile Cruiser, Nephertiti, King Tut Deluxe Sandwich, "Pharoccinos") — a real, evidenced brand angle a generic neighborhood cafe couldn't truthfully claim.

## Operating Context

Dine-in (casual, quiet, good for kids, dogs allowed, outdoor seating), takeout, delivery, and catering. No reservations, no alcohol, no Apple Pay, no Wi-Fi, no TV. Street parking and validated parking available; bike parking available. Closed Mondays; open Tue–Sun 9:00 AM–4:00 PM.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Informational site only — no online ordering, cart, or payment backend (confirmed with Arman; matches every other site in this portfolio). Content sections: hero, menu (with real items/prices only), halal callout, hours/location/contact with map and directions, amenities. Never fabricate menu items, prices, or reviews not present in the source photos.

## Brand Commitments

Name: **Oasis Cafe**. Halal: **explicitly and prominently featured** ("100% Halal Menu" — no specific certifying body was provided, so the claim stays at that level rather than naming a certifier). Existing real logo: gold ring/oval, "OASIS" wordmark in a warm gold/tan, the "Y" stylized as a green palm-frond, "CAFE" in green beneath — supplied as a pasted image (not a saved file in this session, so it is being redrawn as a faithful SVG rather than embedded as a raster export). Real phone: (925) 298-5673. Real address: 3594 Mt Diablo Blvd, Lafayette, CA 94549.

## Evidence on Hand

- Yelp-style listing screenshot: hours, address, phone, full amenities list (delivery, takeout, catering, credit cards, outdoor seating, casual, quiet, good for kids, good for lunch, dogs allowed, street + validated parking, bike parking; explicitly NOT: reservations, Apple Pay, Wi-Fi, TV, good for groups, alcohol).
- Real menu board photos (orange/black boards): bagels, soups, salads, specialty Egyptian-themed drinks (Sahara Sunset, Nile Cruiser, Nephertiti, Oasis Latte), Pharoccinos, espresso drinks, hot drinks, cafe sandwiches (Veggie, Pastrami, Turkey Breast, Smoked Ham & Cheese, Tuna Salad, Italian Combo, Falafel Alladin, King Tut Deluxe) — all with real prices.
- A breakfast/panini whiteboard menu photo was also provided but is too low-resolution to transcribe reliably (small handwriting, "Out" annotations) — its specific items/prices are a stated absence; do not guess at them.
- Real logo image (pasted, not saved as a file — see Brand Commitments).
- Real Yelp listing (yelp.com/biz/oasis-cafe-lafayette): 4.3★ average, 95 reviews — used as the real aggregate rating rather than averaging only the quoted reviews. Four real review quotes (Noelle S., Kim B., Brandye K., L C.) and four real photos (storefront, pastry case, iced latte, outdoor seating) sourced from the listing's public photos; personal/solo customer photos were skipped in favor of place and food shots.

## Product Principles

1. Every menu item and price on the site must trace back to the real photographed menu boards — no invented dishes or prices.
2. Halal status is a first-class fact, not a footnote — it must be visible above the fold, not buried in a FAQ.
3. The Egyptian/oasis theme comes from the business's own real drink names and logo, not an imposed aesthetic — lean into what's already true about the brand rather than a generic "cafe" look.
4. Match the established Arman Digital portfolio pattern (real data, real deploy pipeline, live demo) while keeping the visual system distinct from all 8 prior sites, per the portfolio's own no-reskin rule.
